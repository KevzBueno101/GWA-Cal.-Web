Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 24, 2025

App: GWA Calculator (gwac.vercel.app)
Owner/Developer: Kevin Bueno

1. Introduction

GWA Calculator is a simple tool to compute General Weighted Average (GWA). This Privacy Policy explains how the App processes data entered by users.

2. Information We Process

The App may temporarily process the following data while you use the tool:

  • Subject names
  • Units
  • Grades
  • Values entered in the GUI
  • Excel files uploaded for computation

These inputs are processed only in-memory (RAM) during the active session. The App does not store, save, transmit, or share this data to any server, database, third-party service, or analytics platform.

3. How Data Is Used

Data is used only to perform calculations (compute GWA and display results). Nothing is persisted by the App.

4. No Data Sharing or Third-Party Tracking

The App uses no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. Uploaded files are processed locally and are not uploaded to any server.

5. Data Security

Although data is not stored, the App follows good coding practices to keep processing local and avoid unintended transfers. The developer cannot guarantee security for files stored on your device—always keep backups.

6. Children

The App does not collect personal information and is safe for use by all ages.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. Updates will be posted here with an updated date.
